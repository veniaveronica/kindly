Definition

🇬🇧 English

Hortatory exposition text is a type of text used to persuade the reader or listener to take a particular action, or to change their opinion or attitude about an issue, by presenting strong and logical arguments.

🇮🇩 Bahasa Indonesia

Teks hortatory exposition adalah jenis teks yang digunakan untuk meyakinkan atau mengajak pembaca/pendengar mengambil tindakan tertentu, atau mengubah pendapat dan sikap mereka terhadap suatu isu, dengan menyajikan argumen yang kuat dan logis.

Good to know: Hortatory exposition is one branch of exposition text, alongside analytical exposition. The difference: analytical exposition only argues that something is the case, while hortatory exposition goes further and tells the reader what to do about it. It shows up in speeches, editorials, articles, and even ads.
🎯

Function

🇬🇧 English

Its function is to convince the reader of a particular opinion, using relevant facts and evidence, and to encourage them to take action or shift their attitude — without forcing them.

🇮🇩 Bahasa Indonesia

Fungsinya adalah meyakinkan pembaca tentang suatu pendapat, menggunakan fakta dan bukti yang relevan, serta mendorong mereka untuk bertindak atau mengubah sikap — tanpa memaksa.

In short: the writer isn't just informing — they're inviting the reader to think critically about the arguments, then act on them. That's why hortatory exposition is often used to push for positive social change, like encouraging better behavior online.

🧩

Generic Structure

Three parts, always in this order:

1
Thesis / Introduction

Introduce the issue & state your position

Introduces the topic and hooks the reader's attention, then states the writer's opinion clearly. This opinion becomes the foundation for every argument that follows.

"Netiquette — good manners on the internet — is no longer optional. Every student should practice it seriously."
Ini adalah thesis: pernyataan pendapat yang akan dibela di seluruh teks.
2
Arguments

Back it up, one reason at a time

Presents reasons that support the thesis, usually one per paragraph, backed with facts, data, or logical reasoning — strongest argument often comes first.

"Firstly, rude comments online can cause real emotional harm, since screens don't erase the impact of hurtful words."
Argumen didukung alasan logis, bukan sekadar opini kosong.
3
Recommendation

Call the reader to act

Closes with a call to action or a suggested change in attitude, based on the arguments already made — this is what makes it "hortatory."

"Therefore, we must all pause before we post, and treat every screen name as a real person."
Kalimat ajakan langsung — ciri khas kesimpulan hortatory.
🗣️

Language Features

Key features that make a text hortatory:

1First-Person Point of View
The writer often speaks from a personal, first-person stance ("I", "we"), showing they are directly invested in the issue.
"We must take netiquette seriously if we want the internet to stay a safe space."
Menunjukkan penulis terlibat langsung dengan isu yang dibahas.
2Abstract Nouns
Nouns for ideas or qualities rather than physical things — such as culture, courage, policy, respect, empathy — used to discuss the issue on a conceptual level.
"Online respect starts with a simple sense of empathy."
"Respect" dan "empathy" adalah kata benda abstrak, bukan benda fisik.
3Action & Thinking Verbs
Action verbs (ask, argue, implement, share) describe what should be done. Thinking verbs (consider, reflect, evaluate, realize) show the mental process the writer wants the reader to go through.
"Consider how your words land before you post them."
"Consider" = thinking verb, "post" = action verb.
4Modal Adverbs
Words like surely, certainly, definitely, clearly strengthen the writer's confidence in their own claim.
"Clearly, a single careless comment can ruin someone's entire day."
Menegaskan keyakinan penulis terhadap klaimnya.
5Temporal Connectives
Sequence markers such as firstly, secondly, thirdly, finally organize arguments in order and guide the reader from one point to the next.
"Firstly, online words spread fast. Secondly, they're nearly impossible to erase."
Menandai urutan argumen agar mudah diikuti.
6Evaluative Words
Words that judge quality — effective, efficient, trustworthy, valuable, harmful — used to frame the issue as clearly good or bad.
"A quick apology is far more effective than a defensive reply."
"Effective" menilai sesuatu secara positif.
7Grammar: Present Tense & Passive Voice
Hortatory exposition uses the simple present tense to state general truths, and often the passive voice to keep the focus on the issue rather than on who's doing the action.
"Hurtful comments are shared in seconds and rarely disappear."
Kalimat pasif ("are shared") dan present tense ("rarely disappear").
✅

Characteristics

Quick checklist to recognize a hortatory exposition text:

Clear opinion in the Thesis
Posisi penulis dinyatakan tegas sejak awal.
Multiple supporting arguments
Biasanya lebih dari satu paragraf argumen.
Ends with a call to action
Rekomendasi jelas, bukan hanya rangkuman.
Persuasive, not forceful
Mengajak lewat argumen, bukan perintah.
